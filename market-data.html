Proposed scope for a vendor quote

A personal, non-commercial portfolio website; one operator; anonymous readers; US-listed USD stocks and ETFs, including TQQQ and SOXS; initially daily pricing; public share counts, valuations and weights; retained historical snapshots; CSV/JSON downloads; no raw-data resale or broker execution.

Assessment: daily or publication-time prices should be sufficient for this disclosure format. Compare the smallest Twelve Data external-display product with a Tiingo EOD redistribution quote. Shares and market value together reveal a unit price, so publishing valuations alone must not be treated as a licensing workaround.

Confirm total charges, exchange add-ons, raw versus adjusted prices, corporate actions, timestamps, delay, display versus distribution, derived values, backups, historical retention after cancellation and attribution. Actual account balances or private financial records are not needed for the inquiry.
